FullQuality AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

FullQuality AI ("the app") is a photo and video tool that runs on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your photos and videos never leave your device, and we do not collect personal information.

Photos, videos and files

Everything the app does to your media — editing, enhancing, upscaling, converting and exporting — happens on your device. The app does not upload your photos or videos to any server owned by us or anyone else. Files you keep in the app's Files page are stored only on your device (and in your iCloud backup only if you turn that option on in Settings).

Link downloads

When you paste or share a link, the app fetches that file directly from the website you provided to your device. We do not see or store the links you use.

Captions and AI features

AI enhance, upscaling and background tools run entirely on your device with models bundled in the app; no third-party AI service is used and nothing is sent anywhere. Auto captions use Apple's speech recognition built into iOS. On devices that support it, recognition runs on the device; otherwise iOS may process the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive the audio or the text.

Checking a post

If you paste a link to one of your posts in the Check tab, the app loads that public page on your device to read which video versions are available. The link is not sent to us or stored.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. To confirm your subscription status the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your App Store receipt. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email or media. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Permissions

The app asks for access to your photo library and camera only to open and save your own media. Microphone access is used only if you record video in the app. Local-network access, when requested by the system, is used only for optional sharing features.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not build profiles about you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
