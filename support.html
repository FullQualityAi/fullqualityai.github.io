Common questions

AI Enhance is slow. Enhancement runs entirely on your phone. Long or 4K videos take longer; keep the app in the foreground and plugged in for best speed. "Fast" mode is several times quicker than "Best".

Where are my files? Exports you chose Save → Files for are on the Files tab. They stay on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Subscription and refunds. Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For refunds visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Use Restore purchase (on the paywall or in Settings) if Pro isn't showing after a reinstall.
